Ideology

Secular Principles of Social Justice

பிறப்போக்கும் எல்லா உயிர்க்கும்!

TVK Core Principle

To uphold the individual, social, economic, and political rights of all people living in Tamil Nadu — without reducing society to divisions of religion, caste, colour, race, language, gender, or economic status.

The party's strength, foundation, vitality, and revolutionary declarations lie in its principles. TVK is driven by the core vision that everyone deserves everything — building an equitable society where everything is accessible to everyone.

Read Full Ideology →
🗳️

Democracy

Equal rights for all citizens without discrimination based on race, religion, language, caste, or gender. Upholding democratic freedoms against misuse of authority.

⚖️

Equitable Social Justice

Proportional representation is the true path — not mere reservation. Every section of society must be represented in all fields proportionally.

🤝

Equality

Equality across caste, religion, race, language, economy, and gender. Women, the third gender, and persons with disabilities are equal in all respects.

🕊️

Secularism

A government free from interference in personal religious beliefs, treating people of all faiths — and those without — equally.

🌿

Environmental Protection

Regional development that does not harm nature, while actively addressing ecological and climate crises for future generations.

🗣️

Two-Language Policy

Tamil as the mother tongue; English as the link to the world. Tamil shall be the language of administration, legal proceedings, and governance.

Action Plan

Not Promises — Committed Programs

TVK's action plans are rooted in long-term ideals, serving as a guide to meet today's needs and to shape the future development of Tamil Nadu.

01
🏛️

Administrative Reform

No political interference in government or private sectors. Corruption-free, honest administration guided by caste, religion, and gender neutrality — with progressive, scientific governance.

02
📜

Code of Conduct for Legislators

A formal Code of Conduct will be established and regulated for Members of the Legislative Assembly and Ministers — ensuring accountability at the highest level.

03
🏙️

Decentralised Governance

A branch of the Secretariat will be established in Madurai — making government more accessible to the people of southern Tamil Nadu, similar to the High Court branch model.

04
⚖️

Social Justice in Representation

Proportional representation across all fields until caste inequalities are fully eradicated. Education, healthcare, clean air, and safe drinking water as fundamental rights for all.

05
🌿

Environmental Protection

Protecting the environment and natural resources is the government's foremost duty. Development that does not harm nature — addressing ecological and climate crises with urgency.

06
💊

Drug-Free Tamil Nadu

Building a Tamil Nadu free from intoxicants that damage productivity, health, and well-being — eliminating the root causes of social decay and family breakdown.

Core Principles

Emphatic Views

Driven by the core vision that everyone deserves everything — for the people, with the people, as one among the people.

🧠

Rationalist Mindset

Rejecting regressive ideas that harm the physical, mental, or moral well-being of humankind.

🏛️

State Autonomy

State autonomy is the foremost right of the people. Reclaiming every right that belongs under state autonomy.

🚫

Abolish Untouchability

Rejecting regressive traditions is the first step — complete and permanent eradication of untouchability.

🛡️

Fundamental Rights

Corruption-free administration in public and private sectors. Education, healthcare, clean air, and safe water as rights — not privileges.

Action Plan Focus Areas

Programs for People's Welfare

TVK is committed to creating and implementing programs rooted in long-term ideals — shaping Tamil Nadu's future across every sector.

01

Administrative Reform

Corruption-free, honest governance with scientific methods. No political interference in public or private sectors, guided by neutrality across caste, religion, and gender.

02

Social Justice

Proportional representation in all fields until inequalities are removed and caste is completely eradicated. Education and healthcare as fundamental rights free from discrimination.

03

Language Policy

Tamil as the mother tongue; English as the link language. Tamil shall be the language of administration and legal proceedings. Priority for Tamil-medium educated citizens in government jobs.

04

State Rights

Reclaiming every right that belongs under state autonomy. Opposing anti-people actions that misuse authority and administrative machinery to suppress fundamental freedoms.

05

Women Welfare

Women are equal to men in every sphere. The third gender and persons with disabilities are equal to others in all respects. Dedicated welfare programs across all 38 districts.

06

Education

Quality education as a non-negotiable right — free from discrimination based on religion, race, language, or social class — accessible to every citizen of Tamil Nadu.

07

Healthcare

Universal healthcare as a fundamental right. Clean air and safe drinking water guaranteed for every citizen, free from discrimination on any basis.

08

Agriculture

Sustainable policies protecting Tamil Nadu's farmers — ensuring fair pricing and building resilience against climate and market volatility.

09

Environmental Protection

Regional development that does not harm nature. Protecting the environment and natural resources is the government's foremost duty — addressing ecological and climate crises with urgency.
